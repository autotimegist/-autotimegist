The FORCES Quick Checklist

Remember this acronym for essential monthly checks:

Fuel Oil Rubber Coolant Electrics Screen wash

These checks take 15 minutes and can prevent 90% of common roadside breakdowns.

Winter Maintenance (December - February)

Cold Weather Protection

Winter is hardest on your car's electrical system and rubber components.

  • Test battery voltage and cold-cranking amps
  • Clean corrosion from terminals
  • Check tire pressure weekly
  • Inspect tread depth (minimum 5/32" for snow)
  • Switch to winter tires if in snow belt
Battery fact: Cold weather can reduce battery strength significantly. Test voltage before temperatures drop to avoid morning no-starts.
Tip: For cars parked long-term, use a smart charger (about $50) to maintain battery health. One owner's 3-month storage mistake cost $3,500 in battery replacement.

Spring Maintenance (March - May)

Fluid Checks and Rust Prevention

After winter's harsh conditions, a thorough inspection prevents long-term damage.

  • Check all fluids: engine oil, coolant, transmission, brake, power steering
  • Inspect undercarriage for rust and damage
  • Test battery condition after winter stress
  • Check wiper blades for winter wear
  • Inspect belts and hoses for cracking
Critical: Ice, mud, and road salt trapped in the undercarriage can cause rust and damage suspension components. Inspect thoroughly after winter.

Summer Maintenance (June - August)

Cooling System and AC Prep

Road trip season demands a fully functional cooling system.

  • Inspect radiator hoses for leaks
  • Check coolant levels and condition
  • Test air conditioning system
  • Replace cabin air filter
  • Check tire pressure before long trips

Heat warning: Extreme heat can cause battery fluid to evaporate faster. Have your battery tested if it's nearing 3 years old.

Fall Maintenance (September - November)

Lighting and Winter Prep

Prepare for shorter days and holiday travel.

  • Check all lights: headlights, turn signals, brake lights
  • Inspect belts and hoses
  • Check brake pads and rotors
  • Test heating and defrost systems
  • Verify tire tread depth for winter
Tip: Clean headlight housings and ensure equal light output on both sides. This improves visibility and helps other drivers see you.

Monthly Quick Checks (15 Minutes)

  • Engine oil level
  • Coolant level
  • Tire pressure and visible wear
  • Windscreen washer fluid
  • All exterior lights
  • Wiper blade condition

These checks are especially important for vehicles used frequently or for short journeys.

If you drive under 10,000 km/year, follow these rules:

  • Oil changes: Full synthetic lasts 1 year, regardless of mileage
  • Brake fluid: Change every 2 years—hygroscopic, absorbs moisture even if car sits
  • Filters: Check condition rather than replacing by mileage alone
  • Tires: Replace at 5-6 years regardless of tread—rubber degrades
Pro tip for low-mileage drivers: Under-inflated tires reduce fuel economy by 3% and wear faster. Check monthly.

Dashboard Warning Lights

Never ignore warning lights. If any light stays on and you don't know what it means, seek professional assistance immediately.
DIY tool: An OBD2 scanner ($20-50) can read check engine codes and help identify issues, from a loose gas cap to sensor failures.
