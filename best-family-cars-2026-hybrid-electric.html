Family Car Comparison: Hybrid vs. Electric

TypeProsCons
ElectricLower fuel cost, instant torque, minimal maintenance, tax credits up to $7,500.Higher upfront cost, need home charging, range affected by weather.
HybridNo charging needed, great fuel economy, lower initial price than EVs.Still use gas, more complex drivetrain, no tax credits for standard hybrids.
Our advice: Choose electric if you have home charging and drive less than 300 miles most days. Choose hybrid if you want maximum simplicity, no charging worries, and lower upfront cost.
