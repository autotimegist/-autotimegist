BMW iX vs Lexus RZ 2026: Luxury EV Showdown

CE
Cynthia Ebony
February 17, 2026 · 12 min read

The 2026 BMW iX and Lexus RZ represent distinct approaches to the luxury electric SUV. The iX is BMW's dedicated electric flagship—spacious, powerful, and packed with tech. The Lexus RZ, thoroughly updated for 2026, offers more range, a new F Sport performance variant, and Lexus's legendary refinement at a much lower price. Here's how they compare.

Price & Value: Lexus Is the Bargain

The Lexus RZ starts at $47,295, a whopping $28,000 less than the base BMW iX xDrive45. That's a massive difference. Even the top-tier RZ 550e F Sport at $58,295 undercuts the base iX. The BMW iX, while expensive, offers a more premium interior, greater range, and more power, even at entry level.

Range & Efficiency: BMW Leads

The BMW iX xDrive60 achieves up to 364 miles of range, while the iX xDrive45 offers 312 miles—both besting the Lexus. The Lexus RZ 350e now offers 302 miles (base FWD), a huge improvement, but the dual-motor 450e drops to 261 miles, and the performance-oriented 550e F Sport manages just 225 miles. For long-distance driving, the BMW is the clear winner.

Charging: Lexus Gains Supercharger Access

For 2026, the Lexus RZ comes standard with a NACS port, granting direct access to Tesla's Supercharger network, a massive advantage for road-trippers. The BMW iX retains its CCS port and charges at up to 195 kW (vs. the RZ's 150 kW), meaning faster peak charging speeds where CCS networks are available. Both achieve 10-80% in about 30 minutes.

Interior & Luxury: BMW's Tech vs. Lexus's Simplicity

The BMW iX features a minimalist yet opulent cabin with a curved 14.9-inch display, available Bowers & Wilkins sound system, and exceptionally quiet highway cruising—RAC measured just 65 decibels. The Lexus RZ focuses on simplicity with an 11.4-inch touchscreen and synthetic leather upholstery. It's refined but less lavish. The iX also offers more cargo space (77.9 cu ft max vs. about 18.4 cu ft in the RZ).

Performance: BMW Dominates

The BMW iX M70 produces 650 hp and hits 60 mph in 3.6 seconds. Even the base iX xDrive45 delivers 402 hp and 4.9-second 0-60. The Lexus RZ 550e F Sport offers 402 hp and a 4.1-second 0-60, matching the base BMW's power. The RZ 450e is much slower at 4.9 seconds with only 308 hp. For driving enthusiasts, the BMW is the clear choice.

Awards & Recognition

The BMW iX earned 9/10 from RAC and praise for being "among the best luxury electric cars on sale right now". The Lexus RZ received a 7.5/10 from Car and Driver, with the verdict that "with improved power and range, it's now more compelling than ever".

Quick Verdict: Which Should You Buy?

Choose the 2026 BMW iX if:

  • You prioritize performance and range: even the base model is powerful
  • You want a spacious, tech-forward luxury interior
  • Budget is less of a concern
  • You value a supremely quiet, refined highway cruiser

Choose the 2026 Lexus RZ if:

  • You want the best value in a luxury EV—$28k less than the BMW
  • You need Supercharger access (NACS port is standard)
  • You appreciate Lexus's understated refinement and simplicity
  • You're interested in the new 402-hp F Sport variant
Our Take: The BMW iX is the superior luxury EV in nearly every objective measure—performance, range, interior quality. But it costs a fortune. The Lexus RZ, especially with its 2026 updates, offers incredible value and now includes Supercharger access. If you can afford it, the iX is the ultimate expression of a luxury electric SUV. If you're budget-conscious, the RZ delivers 90% of the refinement at half the price.
