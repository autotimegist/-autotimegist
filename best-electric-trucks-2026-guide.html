Best Electric Trucks 2026: Buyer's Complete Guide

SO

In 2026, the electric truck market is mature, with options for every buyer, from the work-focused Ford F-150 Lightning to the off-road king Rivian R1T. With more range, faster charging, and real-world proven reliability, there's never been a better time to go electric with your pickup. Here's everything you need to know.

Charging: NACS Is Now Standard

NACS Ports for 2026: All 2026 electric trucks from Ford, GM, and Rivian now come with NACS ports, giving direct access to Tesla's Supercharger network. For towing and long hauls, that means more stations on route and far fewer dead chargers.
