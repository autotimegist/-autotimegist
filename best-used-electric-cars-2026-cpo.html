Used EV Buying Guide

Best Used Electric Cars 2026: Certified Pre-Owned EVs

CO
Charity Ozor
February 28, 2026 · 10 min read

Why Buy Used Electric in 2026? Electric vehicles depreciate faster than gas cars, which means incredible deals for savvy buyers. A 3-year-old EV can cost 40-50% less than new, often with most of its battery warranty intact. And with certified pre-owned (CPO) programs, you get manufacturer-backed peace of mind. Here are the best used EVs to buy in 2026.

Used EV Tax Credit – Up to $4,000: The Inflation Reduction Act offers a tax credit of 30% of the sale price (up to $4,000) for used EVs under $25,000. Income limits apply: $75,000 single, $150,000 joint. The vehicle must be at least 2 years old.

What to Check When Buying a Used EV

Battery Health is Everything: Most EVs lose only 5-10% of range after 100,000 miles. Ask for the current State of Health (SOH) reading—many cars display it in the infotainment system. Also, check that the battery is still under its original 8-year/100,000-mile warranty.

Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) Checklist:
  • Manufacturer-backed warranty (typically 1-2 years/unlimited miles)
  • Multi-point inspection (including battery health check)
  • Vehicle history report (clean title, no accidents)
  • Remaining original battery warranty (8-year/100,000-mile transferable)
  • Roadside assistance included

Used EV Buyer's Checklist

  • Verify battery State of Health (ask dealer or use OBD scanner)
  • Check remaining battery warranty (8-year/100,000-mile federal requirement)
  • Confirm CPO program details and included warranties
  • Check if the car qualifies for the $4,000 used EV tax credit
  • Ensure all recalls have been performed (NHTSA website)
  • Test DC fast charging if possible to ensure proper function
